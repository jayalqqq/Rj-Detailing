About RJ

Built on Pride.
Driven by Detail.

Hi, I'm RJ — and I started this business because I genuinely love cars. What began as detailing vehicles for friends and family turned into a full-time passion.

I got tired of seeing people pay good money at car washes only to drive away with streaky windows and damp seats. You deserve better than that.

RJ Detailing is fully mobile — I bring everything to you. Serving Pleasanton, Walnut Creek, Danville, Concord, Dublin, San Ramon, and surrounding cities across the East Bay.

No cutting corners. No rushing. Just results you can see.
